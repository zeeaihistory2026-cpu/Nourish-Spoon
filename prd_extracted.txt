1. Product Overview
Nourish Spoon is a premium Pakistani food brand centered on handcrafted Panjeeri and Date & Nuts Energy Balls. The mobile application must preserve the website’s brand identity, content hierarchy and WhatsApp-first ordering model while improving discoverability, product education and repeat engagement. The admin dashboard must act as the operational control center for all app-managed content and settings.
Item
Requirement
Primary objective
Deliver a polished, mobile-first shopping and brand experience that mirrors Nourish Spoon’s existing website and business flow.
Business model
Product discovery and WhatsApp-assisted ordering, with optional native cart/checkout added later.
Primary users
Customers in Pakistan, Nourish Spoon administrators and staff.
Core principle
App content must be dynamic and controlled from the admin dashboard rather than hard-coded.
2. Goals and Success Criteria
Keep mobile app content, pricing, reviews, product information and business policies aligned with the live Nourish Spoon website.
Make the primary buying path fast: discover product, select size, review details, initiate WhatsApp order.
Allow admins to update products, pricing, variants, content, reviews, FAQ, delivery, payment and appearance without app releases.
Provide a scalable technical foundation for native checkout, payment gateways, loyalty and push notifications later.
Maintain strong accessibility, readable typography, responsive layouts and consistent light/dark themes.

10. Non-Functional Requirements
10.1 Performance
Lazy-load non-critical images.
Compress images and use efficient formats where supported.
Cache API responses with TanStack Query.
Paginate reviews and admin order lists.
Use skeleton loaders for perceived performance.
Minimize startup network requests.
Use optimistic updates for appropriate admin operations.
10.2 Accessibility
Readable type scale and sufficient color contrast.
Minimum touch target sizes appropriate for mobile platforms.
Semantic labels for icons and controls.
Support Dynamic Type/font scaling where practical.
Do not communicate status using color alone.
10.3 Security
Supabase Row Level Security.
Role-based admin permissions.
Secure environment variables.
Server-side validation for privileged actions.
Zod validation on forms.
Rate limiting for contact/inquiry endpoints where applicable.
Image/file validation.
Secure authentication sessions.
Audit logs for critical admin actions.
10.4 Reliability
Graceful empty/error states.
Retry transient network failures.
Offline-friendly cached catalog where practical.
Sentry monitoring for production crashes and errors.
11. Design System Requirements
Visual language must closely match Nourish Spoon website: warm cream surfaces, deep natural green, restrained gold accents, botanical motifs and premium food photography.
Use an elegant display face for major headings and a highly readable sans-serif for body/UI labels.
Avoid excessive decorative elements inside transactional flows.
Primary CTA uses deep green; gold is an accent, not the primary action color.
Consistent border radius, spacing scale and elevation tokens across mobile and admin.
Dark mode should preserve hierarchy and warmth rather than simply invert colors.
12. Content Governance
Website and app should share the same factual product content.
Do not invent new health claims or unsupported product benefits.
Prices, review counts and business policies must be admin-managed and treated as volatile data.
Contact details and business hours must be managed centrally.
Legal/health-sensitive copy should support disclaimers where appropriate.

WhatsApp ordering: order form, prefilled message generation and business rules.
Content: reviews, About, FAQ, contact and delivery/payment information.
Admin: dashboard, products, content, reviews, delivery, payments and app settings.
QA: responsive testing, theme testing, accessibility, performance and release preparation.
Post-MVP: native checkout, payments, customer accounts, tracking, push, loyalty and advanced analytics.
15. Product Decisions and Constraints
WhatsApp is the MVP transactional priority because it matches the current website and operational model.
Do not overbuild native checkout before the business explicitly adopts that process.
Product data must not be hard-coded into the app.
A cart icon may be present only if the feature is enabled or clearly planned; otherwise omit it to avoid misleading users.
Admin is the authoritative source for volatile data such as prices, availability, review visibility, delivery options and promotional banners.
